Advertising and privacy

Updated October 30, 2026. Silly Ventures LLC operates this public information site and the separate Slide application.

Public pages and advertising

Google AdSense may display advertising on designated public Slide information pages on sillyventures.co. Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, network information such as your IP address, and device or browser information to deliver, measure and protect advertising services. Where required, Google's consent message lets you choose whether to allow advertising purposes. Availability of ads depends on your choices, browser settings and Google's approval and serving decisions.

Learn how Google uses information from partner sites and manage Google advertising preferences. Consent controls shown on advertising pages can be used to revisit your choices.

The local presentation workspace

The editor at slide.sillyventures.co uses a separate browser origin and does not include AdSense scripts. In local mode, source files are parsed on your device and model inference runs locally. Downloading the application and model assets still makes network requests. Optional analytics, hosted generation, account features and publishing have separate behavior described in the Slide application privacy policy.

We do not send source notes, selected files or presentation content to the advertising page, and do not use them to target ads. Offline HTML presentations do not contain advertising or analytics code. Editable project backups include source material, so treat them as private files.
